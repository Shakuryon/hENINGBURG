Power-Ups · Why Wash

6 reasons to pressure wash your property in Mobile, AL

Pressure washing is more than making things look nice. On the Gulf Coast it is basic upkeep, like changing the oil in your truck.

1

It protects your home's value

Your home is likely the biggest investment you own. Dirt, mold and mildew that sit on siding, brick and trim slowly break those surfaces down and drag down curb appeal. A regular house washing keeps the exterior looking the way it did the day it was built, which matters when it is time to sell, refinance or rent.

2

It stops mold, mildew and algae at the root

Mobile stays humid all year, so algae, mildew and mold grow fast on roofs, stucco, vinyl siding and wood. Rinsing off the top layer only buys a few months. The right detergents kill the growth down to the root, so your house stays clean longer and you are not paying for another wash before the year is out.

3

It makes driveways and walkways safer

Algae and Alabama's orange clay turn concrete slick, and it gets worse after a hard rain. Driveway cleaning and sidewalk cleaning remove that film, so your family, your guests and your customers have solid footing on the way to the door.

4

It preps surfaces for paint, stain and sealer

Paint and stain hold best on a clean surface. Washing a deck, fence or house exterior before a project clears away the grime, chalk and loose material that cause a new finish to peel early. If you are planning to repaint or restain, start with a cleaning.

5

It keeps your business image clean

Customers and tenants judge a property before they walk in. Clean storefronts, sidewalks, apartment buildings and school walkways show that the place is cared for. Commercial pressure washing in Mobile keeps your entrances, breezeways and parking areas looking sharp. You have an image, we keep it clean!

6

A yearly wash costs less than repairs

How often should you pressure wash in Mobile? For most homes, once a year keeps growth to a minimum. Concrete driveways can usually go a little longer between cleanings. A set maintenance plan is cheaper than replacing rotted wood or stained siding later, and we will build yours for free when we come out for your estimate.
